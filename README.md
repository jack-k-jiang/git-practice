### AI Safety Requirements
The article I chose is from The Guardian talking about necessity of AI safety, especially due to recent developments, and what safety truly entails: [AI safety requires more than just slowing our pace](https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2026/sep/15/ai-safety-requirements).

### Why this article is interesting

After OpenAI allegedly solved the Navier-Stokes problem, it is undeniable that AI progress is beyond what most people have imagined. As such, there has now been huge talks about enforcing guardrails and investing in AI safety. For example, Anthropic CEO Dario, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, and other influential figures have all agreed that there needs to be some sort of slowdown.

The author of the article, Russell, propose the following safety guidelines:
- The U.S. government must catch up and set mandatory safety guardrails
- If models are not safe, development must stop
- There needs to be international agreements so that safety is standardized across the globe

Overall, I am incredibly interested in the progress of AI and how AI safety will evolve with it.
