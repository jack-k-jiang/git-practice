 ## My Thoughts
The article above is a research experiment Anthropic conducts on software engineers to see the effect of AI assistance on their coding skills and understanding. All the participants had to complete a coding task using an unfamiliar Python package, and next had to take a quiz on the coding concepts they just used. As expected, there was an overall decrease in quiz score when engineers relied on AI assistance. However, what I found interesting was that some participants asked the AI to generate the code, but also asked follow up questions to improve their understanding, and others only asked conceptual questions to help them complete the task. These participants relied on AI yet scored higher on the quiz, 

## Comment from Selma Nahas
What I found really interesting is how AI may help people work faster when they already know what they are doing, but could make it harder to develop skills they are still learning. It also makes me think about how AI itself is still developing, yet people are already relying on it so heavily. Instead of using AI as an aid, we may be depending on it too much this early in its development. It will be interesting to see how this affects the way people learn and build skills over time.
